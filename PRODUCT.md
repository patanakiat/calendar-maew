# Product Brief

Calendar Maew is a production-ready availability calendar and scheduling tool for teams from small groups to large organizations.

## Goals
- Let users mark dates as available or unavailable with a reason.
- Show a clear team-wide overview of availability in one place.
- Highlight dates when everyone is available.
- Support real multi-user data with persistent storage and authentication.

## Users
- Small teams coordinating meetings
- Cross-functional groups planning events
- Larger organizations needing availability visibility

## Core flows
1. Authenticate.
2. Select a date on an interactive calendar.
3. Mark available or unavailable and attach a reason.
4. Review the team overview grid/heatmap.
5. Identify days when everyone is available.

## Scope guardrails
- No extra product features beyond availability scheduling.
- No unnecessary abstractions.
- Keep the interface fast, clear, and responsive.

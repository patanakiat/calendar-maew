04 /Designed to coexist

A commons, not a free-for-all.
The boundaries make it work.

A space is someone’s livelihood before it’s anyone’s possibility. Hosts set the boundaries. Every proposed match needs an agreed use, a named steward, and a clear handover.

Access, accessibility, insurance, and reset time are checked before a space opens. Sharing should add to a neighborhood, not ask it to absorb the cost.
